About Christopher

Professional Biography

Christopher Sebastian is an independent architect and designer specializing in residential and commercial design. His international background spans Italy, New Zealand, Singapore, Germany, and the United States, informing his appreciation for different cultures, environments, and approaches to design.

Working directly with clients in the United States, Christopher develops design solutions that balance creative vision, functionality, spatial planning, and attention to detail. His approach focuses on understanding each client's needs and translating their ideas into thoughtful, practical spaces.

Academic Background

Education & Design Perspective

Christopher pursued architectural studies at the University of Auckland in New Zealand. His academic experience helped develop his design perspective, creative thinking, and approach to architectural problem-solving.

His design philosophy emphasizes clear communication, professional responsibility, functional planning, and spaces that unite visual quality with practical purpose.
