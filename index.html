01 / About

Design shaped by people, purpose and place.

Christopher Sebastian is an independent architect and designer with an international outlook and an enduring interest in the relationship between thoughtful design and everyday living.

Born in Italy, my life and experiences have taken me through New Zealand, Singapore, Germany and the United States. Living across different places has broadened my perspective on architecture, culture and the many ways people make a space their own.

My approach balances creativity with practical thinking. I believe successful design should respond to its setting, make intelligent use of space and serve the people who will live or work in it.

I value clear communication, careful planning and a considered approach to each project, from the first ideas through design development and presentation.

02 / Practice

Considered from the beginning.
