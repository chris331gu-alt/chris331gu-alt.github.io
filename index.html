01 / About

Design begins
with people.

Good design is more than appearance. It brings together practical needs, the character of a place, and the small details that make a space work beautifully every day.

“Thoughtful design. Practical solutions. Spaces made to be lived in.”

Christopher Sebastian is an independent architect and designer working across residential and commercial design. His approach combines creative thinking with practical planning and a close attention to how people experience the spaces around them.

With an international outlook shaped by living and studying in different parts of the world, Christopher values clear communication, considered decisions, and design that responds to each client's needs and the context of each project.

Every project begins with listening: understanding the brief, exploring possibilities, and working toward a result that feels both purposeful and enduring.
