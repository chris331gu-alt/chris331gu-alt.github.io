Residential Architecture

Bellevue Kitchen Renovation

Bellevue, Washington

A thoughtful transformation of a 1960s split-entry home, reimagining an underused kitchen and adjoining spaces as a welcoming, light-filled heart of the home.

Natural light, open volume and warm materials define the renovated kitchen.

Reimagining the Heart of the Home

This renovation reconfigured the existing kitchen to create a more generous and connected living environment. Removing two walls opened the kitchen and dining area into an underused adjoining room, strengthening the relationship with the living room and improving the flow between everyday activities.

Above, a vaulted ceiling makes use of previously unused attic space, giving the kitchen a greater sense of height and openness. Two skylights introduce natural daylight, helping the interior feel brighter and more inviting throughout the day.

Warm Materials and Thoughtful Details

Custom walnut cabinetry establishes a rich natural character, balanced by light quartz countertops and a Heath Ceramics backsplash. A walnut butcher-block bar top adds another layer of warmth, while a generous kitchen island provides a practical centre for food preparation, informal dining and gathering.

A custom steel chimney wrap and gas fireplace insert contribute a refined focal point. Engineered hardwood flooring replaces the former carpet and linoleum, while updated trim, paint and lighting bring consistency to the renewed interior.

The result is a more cohesive and functional home, shaped around natural light, comfortable movement, durable finishes and the simple pleasure of spending time together.
